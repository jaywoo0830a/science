## How to Use These Examples

1. **Read the problem.** Do not look at the solution.
2. **Apply the pre-method** (coordinate system template, variable table with dimensions).
3. **Execute I-SEE-R** step by step on your own paper.
4. **Compare your trace** to the solution. Identify exactly which decision differed.
5. **If your decision was different**, determine whether it was equivalent (different coordinate system, same physical result) or erroneous.

Each solution uses the LaTeX conventions of the latest KaTeX-compatible standard.
