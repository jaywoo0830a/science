## 1. How many moles in $50.0 \ \text{g}$ of $\ce{CaCO3}$?

$$
M(\ce{CaCO3}) = 40.08 + 12.01 + 3(16.00) = 100.09 \ \text{g/mol}
$$
$$
n = \dfrac{50.0}{100.09} = \boxed{0.500 \ \text{mol}}
$$

*Pattern: $n = m/M$ (Stoichiometry 1)*

## 2. Gas at $3.00 \ \text{atm}$, $2.00 \ \text{L}$. Pressure increases to $9.00 \ \text{atm}$ at constant $T$. New volume?

$$
P_1 V_1 = P_2 V_2
$$
$$
3.00 \times 2.00 = 9.00 \times V_2
$$
$$
V_2 = \dfrac{6.00}{9.00} = \boxed{0.667 \ \text{L}}
$$

*Pattern: Boyle's Law, constant $T$ (Gas Laws 1)*

## 5. $75.0 \ \text{g}$ water ($c = 4.184$) heated from $20.0^\circ\text{C}$ to $45.0^\circ\text{C}$. Heat absorbed (kJ)?

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta T &= 45.0 - 20.0 = 25.0^\circ\text{C} \\
q &= mc\Delta T = 75.0 \times 4.184 \times 25.0 = 7845 \ \text{J} = \boxed{7.85 \ \text{kJ}}
\end{aligned}
$$

*Pattern: $q = mc\Delta T$ (Thermochemistry 1)*

## 6. Find the rate law and $k$.

| Expt | $[\ce{A}]$ | $[\ce{B}]$ | Rate (M/s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | $0.10$ | $0.10$ | $2.0 \times 10^{-4}$ |
| 2 | $0.20$ | $0.10$ | $8.0 \times 10^{-4}$ |
| 3 | $0.10$ | $0.20$ | $4.0 \times 10^{-4}$ |

Compare 1 $\to$ 2: $[\ce{A}]$ doubles, $[\ce{B}]$ constant. Rate: $8.0\times10^{-4} / 2.0\times10^{-4} = 4.0 = 2^m \Rightarrow m = 2$.

Compare 1 $\to$ 3: $[\ce{B}]$ doubles, $[\ce{A}]$ constant. Rate: $4.0\times10^{-4} / 2.0\times10^{-4} = 2.0 = 2^n \Rightarrow n = 1$.

$$
\boxed{\text{rate} = k[\ce{A}]^2[\ce{B}]^1}
$$

$$
k = \dfrac{2.0\times10^{-4}}{(0.10)^2(0.10)} = \dfrac{2.0\times10^{-4}}{1.0\times10^{-3}} = \boxed{0.20 \ \text{M}^{-2}\!\cdot\!\text{s}^{-1}}
$$

*Pattern: Initial rates method (Kinetics 1)*

## 9. Gas at $127^\circ\text{C}$, $4.00 \ \text{L}$ at constant $P$. Cooled to $27^\circ\text{C}$. New volume?

$$
\begin{aligned}
T_1 &= 127 + 273 = 400 \ \text{K} \\
T_2 &= 27 + 273 = 300 \ \text{K} \\[4pt]
\dfrac{V_1}{T_1} &= \dfrac{V_2}{T_2} \\[4pt]
\dfrac{4.00}{400} &= \dfrac{V_2}{300} \\[4pt]
V_2 &= 4.00 \cdot \dfrac{300}{400} = \boxed{3.00 \ \text{L}}
\end{aligned}
$$

*Pattern: Charles's Law, constant $P$ (Gas Laws 2)*

## 10. $0.100 \ \text{M} \ \ce{CH3COOH}$ ($K_a = 1.8\times10^{-5}$). $[\ce{H+}]$?

ICE: $\ce{CH3COOH <=> CH3COO- + H+}$. $x = [\ce{H+}]$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
K_a &= \dfrac{x^2}{0.100 - x} = 1.8\times10^{-5} \\[4pt]
\dfrac{0.100}{1.8\times10^{-5}} &= 5556 > 1000 \quad \checkmark \ \text{(small-}x) \\[4pt]
x &= \sqrt{0.100 \times 1.8\times10^{-5}} = \sqrt{1.8\times10^{-6}} = \boxed{1.34\times10^{-3} \ \text{M}}
\end{aligned}
$$

*Pattern: Weak acid $K_a$ (Acid-Base 3)*

## 11. $\ce{N2 + 3H2 <=> 2NH3}$ at $400^\circ\text{C}$, $K_c = 0.50$. Find $K_p$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta n &= 2 - (1+3) = -2 \\
T &= 400 + 273 = 673 \ \text{K} \\[4pt]
K_p &= K_c(RT)^{\Delta n} = 0.50 \times (0.08206 \times 673)^{-2} \\[4pt]
&= 0.50 \times (55.23)^{-2} = \dfrac{0.50}{3050} = \boxed{1.64\times10^{-4}}
\end{aligned}
$$

*Pattern: $K_p$ from $K_c$ (Equilibrium 2)*

## 29. $\ce{2Al + 3Cl2 -> 2AlCl3}$. $5.40 \ \text{g} \ \ce{Al}$ + $21.3 \ \text{g} \ \ce{Cl2}$. Limiting? Grams $\ce{AlCl3}$? Excess remaining?

$$
\begin{aligned}
n_{\ce{Al}} &= \dfrac{5.40}{26.98} = 0.2001 \ \text{mol} \quad\Rightarrow\quad \dfrac{0.2001}{2} = 0.1001 \\[4pt]
n_{\ce{Cl2}} &= \dfrac{21.3}{70.90} = 0.3004 \ \text{mol} \quad\Rightarrow\quad \dfrac{0.3004}{3} = 0.1001
\end{aligned}
$$

Both equal -- **neither is limiting** (exactly stoichiometric).

$$
\begin{aligned}
n_{\ce{AlCl3}} &= 0.2001 \cdot \dfrac{2}{2} = 0.2001 \ \text{mol} \\
M(\ce{AlCl3}) &= 26.98 + 3(35.45) = 133.33 \ \text{g/mol} \\
m_{\ce{AlCl3}} &= 0.2001 \times 133.33 = \boxed{26.7 \ \text{g}}
\end{aligned}
$$

Excess remaining: $\boxed{0 \ \text{g}}$ (both consumed completely).

*Pattern: Limiting reagent -- equal ratio edge case (Stoichiometry 5)*

[\ce{HI}] &= 2 \times 0.393 = \boxed{0.786 \ \text{M}}
\end{aligned}
$$

*Pattern: ICE $\to$ equilibrium $[]$ from $K$ (Equilibrium 4)*

## 42. $E_{\text{cell}}$ for $\ce{Zn + Cu^{2+}(0.010 M) -> Zn^{2+}(1.00 M) + Cu}$. $E^\circ = 1.10 \ \text{V}$, $n = 2$. (Nernst)

$$
\begin{aligned}

&= 1.10 - \dfrac{0.0592}{2} \times \log(100) \\[4pt]
&= 1.10 - 0.0296 \times 2 = 1.10 - 0.0592 = \boxed{1.04 \ \text{V}}
\end{aligned}
$$

## 44. $0.400 \ \text{mol} \ \ce{N2} + 0.600 \ \text{mol} \ \ce{O2}$ in $8.00 \ \text{L}$ at $27^\circ\text{C}$. Total pressure? Partial pressures?

## 46. $K_c = 54.0$ for $\ce{H2 + I2 <=> 2HI}$. Current: $[\ce{H2}] = 0.100$, $[\ce{I2}] = 0.100$, $[\ce{HI}] = 1.50$. Which way shifts?

## 47. Estimate $\Delta H$ for $\ce{H2 + Cl2 -> 2HCl}$ using bond enthalpies.

BE: $\ce{H-H} = 436$, $\ce{Cl-Cl} = 243$, $\ce{H-Cl} = 431 \ \text{kJ/mol}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sum\text{BE}_{\text{broken}} &= 436 + 243 = 679 \ \text{kJ} \\
\sum\text{BE}_{\text{formed}} &= 2 \times 431 = 862 \ \text{kJ} \\[4pt]
\Delta H &= 679 - 862 = \boxed{-183 \ \text{kJ}}
\end{aligned}
$$

*Pattern: Bond enthalpy (Thermochemistry 5)*

## 48. $k_1 = 2.0\times10^{-4}$ at $25^\circ\text{C}$, $k_2 = 8.0\times10^{-4}$ at $45^\circ\text{C}$. $E_a$ (kJ/mol)?

$$
\begin{aligned}
T_1 &= 298 \ \text{K}, \quad T_2 = 318 \ \text{K} \\[4pt]
\ln\left(\dfrac{k_2}{k_1}\right) &= \dfrac{E_a}{R}\left(\dfrac{1}{T_1} - \dfrac{1}{T_2}\right) \\[4pt]
\ln\left(\dfrac{8.0\times10^{-4}}{2.0\times10^{-4}}\right) &= \dfrac{E_a}{8.314}\left(\dfrac{1}{298} - \dfrac{1}{318}\right) \\[4pt]
\ln(4.0) = 1.3863 &= \dfrac{E_a}{8.314} \times (0.003356 - 0.003145) \\[4pt]
1.3863 &= \dfrac{E_a}{8.314} \times 0.000211 \\[4pt]
E_a &= \dfrac{1.3863 \times 8.314}{0.000211} = 54{,}600 \ \text{J/mol} = \boxed{54.6 \ \text{kJ/mol}}
\end{aligned}
$$

*Pattern: Arrhenius two-point (Kinetics 6)*

## 52. $0.050 \ \text{M} \ \ce{NH3}$ ($K_b = 1.8\times10^{-5}$). pH?

ICE: $\ce{NH3 + H2O <=> NH4+ + OH-}$. $x = [\ce{OH-}]$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
K_b &= \dfrac{x^2}{0.050 - x} \approx \dfrac{x^2}{0.050} \quad (\text{small-}x) \\[4pt]
x &= \sqrt{0.050 \times 1.8\times10^{-5}} = \sqrt{9.0\times10^{-7}} = 9.49\times10^{-4} \\[4pt]
\text{pOH} &= -\log(9.49\times10^{-4}) = 3.02 \\[4pt]
\text{pH} &= 14.00 - 3.02 = \boxed{10.98}
\end{aligned}
$$

*Pattern: Weak base $K_b$ (Acid-Base 4)*

## 53. $\ce{NH4HS(s) <=> NH3(g) + H2S(g)}$. Total pressure above solid $= 0.500 \ \text{atm}$. Find $K_p$.

K_p = P_{\ce{NH3}} \cdot P_{\ce{H2S}} = 0.250 \times 0.250 = \boxed{0.0625}
$$

*Pattern: Heterogeneous equilibrium (Equilibrium 8)*

## 54. $100.0 \ \text{mL}$ of $0.500 \ \text{M} \ \ce{HCl}$ + $100.0 \ \text{mL}$ of $0.500 \ \text{M} \ \ce{NaOH}$. $T$ rises $22.0^\circ\text{C} \to 25.4^\circ\text{C}$. $d = 1.00$, $c = 4.184$. $\Delta H$ per mol $\ce{H2O}$?

\end{aligned}
$$

*Pattern: Coffee cup calorimetry (Thermochemistry 6)*

## 55. $E_a = 65 \ \text{kJ/mol}$, $k = 0.020 \ \text{s}^{-1}$ at $300 \ \text{K}$. $k$ at $350 \ \text{K}$?

$$
\begin{aligned}
\ln\left(\dfrac{k_2}{k_1}\right) &= \dfrac{E_a}{R}\left(\dfrac{1}{T_1} - \dfrac{1}{T_2}\right) \\[4pt]

&= 7819 \times 0.000476 = 3.723 \\[4pt]
\dfrac{k_2}{0.020} &= e^{3.723} = 41.4 \\[4pt]
k_2 &= 0.020 \times 41.4 = \boxed{0.83 \ \text{s}^{-1}}
\end{aligned}
$$

*Pattern: Arrhenius equation (Kinetics 6)*

## 60. $25.0 \ \text{mL}$ of $0.100 \ \text{M} \ \ce{CH3COOH}$ ($K_a = 1.8\times10^{-5}$) + $12.5 \ \text{mL}$ of $0.100 \ \text{M} \ \ce{NaOH}$. pH?
