# BASIC — 50 Problems (Level 1–2, 1–3 cards)

## M1–M8: Algebra & Functions

**M1.** Solve $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$ using the quadratic formula. [Cards: 1]

**M2.** Determine the nature of the roots of $2x^2 - 4x + 3 = 0$ using the discriminant. [Cards: 2]

**M3.** Without solving, find the sum and product of the roots of $3x^2 - 7x + 2 = 0$. [Cards: 3]

**M4.** Factor completely: $x^4 - 16$. [Cards: 4]

**M5.** Solve $2^{x} = 32$. [Cards: 15, 22]

**M6.** Expand $\log_3(9x^2)$ using logarithm properties. [Cards: 19]

**M7.** Find the domain of $f(x) = \dfrac{\sqrt{x-2}}{x-5}$. [Cards: 25]

**M8.** Write $y = x^2 + 6x + 5$ in vertex form and state the vertex. [Cards: 8, 9]

## M9–M15: Trigonometry

**M11.** Find the arc length of a sector with radius $10\;\mathrm{cm}$ and central angle $\pi/3$ radians. [Cards: 51]

**M12.** Evaluate $\sin(135^\circ)$ using the unit circle and reference angle. [Cards: 48, 54]

**M13.** Simplify $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta + \tan^2\theta$ using identities. [Cards: 56]

**M14.** Find all solutions of $\cos x = 1/2$ for $x \in [0, 2\pi)$. [Cards: 69]

**M15.** State the amplitude and period of $y = 3\sin(2x)$. [Cards: 71]

**M20.** Is $f(x) = \begin{cases} x^2 & x < 1 \\ 2x - 1 & x \ge 1 \end{cases}$ continuous at $x = 1$? [Cards: 86]

## M21–M30: Derivatives

**M25.** Find the derivative of $y = \tan(2x)$. [Cards: 97, 100]

**M26.** Find the equation of the tangent line to $f(x) = x^3 - x$ at $x = 2$. [Cards: 120]

**M27.** Find all critical points of $f(x) = x^3 - 3x + 1$. [Cards: 111]

**M28.** Determine where $f(x) = x^3 - 3x$ is increasing. [Cards: 285]

**M30.** Use the limit definition to find $f'(x)$ for $f(x) = x^2$. [Cards: 91]

## M31–M38: Integrals

**M31.** Evaluate $\displaystyle\int (3x^2 + 2x - 1)\,dx$. [Cards: 125, 93, 94]

**M32.** Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \sin x\,dx$. [Cards: 129]

**M33.** Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^2 (x^2 + 1)\,dx$. [Cards: 125, 148]

## M39–M42: Sequences & Series

## M47–M50: Probability & Statistics

**M70.** Water pours into a conical tank (height $4\;\mathrm{m}$, radius $2\;\mathrm{m}$) at $2\;\mathrm{m^3/min}$. How fast is the water level rising when the water is $1\;\mathrm{m}$ deep? [Cards: 108, 290]

**M71.** Find the point on the parabola $y = x^2$ closest to the point $(0, 3)$. [Cards: 1, 117]

## M141–M150: Advanced Applications

**M141.** The error function is defined as $\operatorname{erf}(x) = \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{\pi}}\displaystyle\int_0^x e^{-t^2}\,dt$. Find $\dfrac{d}{dx}\operatorname{erf}(x^2)$. [Cards: 97, 147, 150, 308]

**M142.** Evaluate the Gaussian integral $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^\infty e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}$ by squaring and converting to polar coordinates. (Set up the double integral; the evaluation proves the result.) [Cards: 158, 207, 208, 308, 310]

**M143.** A bead slides down a frictionless wire from $(0,0)$ to $(a,b)$ under gravity. Find the shape $y(x)$ that minimizes descent time. (This is the brachistochrone problem — set up the integral and derive the cycloid solution.) [Cards: 117, 156, 205, 309]

**M144.** The probability density function of the standard normal distribution is $\phi(x) = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-x^2/2}$. Show that $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^\infty \phi(x)\,dx = 1$. [Cards: 158, 236, 142]

**M145.** Compute the volume of the 4-dimensional unit ball: $V_4 = \{(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4) : x_1^2+x_2^2+x_3^2+x_4^2 \le 1\}$. (Hint: iterate integrals and use trigonometric substitution.) [Cards: 142, 153, 155]

**M146.** Find the centroid $(\bar{x}, \bar{y})$ of the region bounded by $y = \sin x$ and $y = 0$ from $x = 0$ to $x = \pi$. [Cards: 130, 148, 151, 152]

**M147.** The function $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{1+x^2}$ is expanded in a power series about $x=0$. What is the interval of convergence? Why does the series diverge at $x=1$ despite $f(1) = 1/2$ being perfectly well-defined? [Cards: 178, 185, 253]

**M148.** Evaluate the Frullani integral: $\displaystyle\int_0^\infty \dfrac{e^{-ax} - e^{-bx}}{x}\,dx$ for $a, b > 0$. [Cards: 141, 158, 308]

**M149.** Derive Wallis's product: $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty} \dfrac{2\cdot2\cdot4\cdot4\cdots(2n)(2n)}{1\cdot3\cdot3\cdot5\cdots(2n-1)(2n+1)} = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ by evaluating $\int_0^{\pi/2} \sin^n x\,dx$. [Cards: 145, 314]

**M150.** Prove the irrationality of $e$: Suppose $e = p/q$ with integers $p,q$. Use the series $e = \sum_{k=0}^\infty 1/k!$ and the estimate $0 < q!(e - \sum_{k=0}^q 1/k!) < 1/q$ to reach a contradiction. [Cards: 181, 188, 307]
