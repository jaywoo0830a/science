# Quick Reference — The 12 Most Common Pitfalls

| # | Pitfall | Consequence | Card |
|---|---------|-------------|------|
| 1 | Temperature not in Kelvin | Gas law, thermodynamics, Arrhenius all break | 197 |
| 2 | $\Delta H$ (kJ) vs $\Delta S$ (J) unit mismatch in $\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S$ | $\Delta G$ off by factor of 1000 | 48 |
| 3 | Wrong $R$ for pressure unit (0.08206 vs 8.314) | $PV=nRT$ gives nonsense | 20 |
| 4 | Pure solids/liquids included in $K$ expression | $K$ value is wrong | 89 |
| 5 | Small-$K$ approximation used without 5% verification | May need quadratic instead | 94 |
| 6 | Molar mass in g/mol used in $v_{\text{rms}} = \sqrt{3RT/M}$ | $v_{\text{rms}}$ wrong by factor of ~32 | 30 |
| 7 | $\Delta n$ in $K_p = K_c(RT)^{\Delta n}$ counts all species, not gases only | $K_p$ is wrong | 90 |
| 8 | Forgetting that only temperature changes $K$ | Misapplied Le Chatelier | 99 |
| 9 | Henderson-Hasselbalch used when $[\mathrm{A}^-]/[\mathrm{HA}] \ll 0.1$ or $\gg 10$ | pH estimate unreliable | 112 |
| 10 | Equivalence point pH assumed = 7 for weak acid/base titrations | pH off by several units | 115, 116 |
| 11 | van't Hoff factor $i$ omitted for ionic solutes in colligative properties | $\Delta T_b$, $\Delta T_f$, $\Pi$ all too small | 144 |
| 12 | Oxidation number of O assumed −2 in peroxides ($\mathrm{H_2O_2}$: −1) or with F ($\mathrm{OF_2}$: +2) | Redox balancing wrong | 122 |
