## The 12 Most Common Pitfalls (Cards 20–144)

| # | Pitfall | Consequence | Card |
|---|---------|-------------|------|
| 1 | T not in Kelvin | Gas law, thermo, Arrhenius all break | 197 |
| 2 | $\Delta H$ (kJ) vs $\Delta S$ (J) mismatch in $\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S$ | $\Delta G$ off by ×1000 | 48 |
| 3 | Wrong $R$ for pressure unit | $PV=nRT$ gives nonsense | 20 |
| 4 | Solids/liquids in $K$ expression | $K$ value wrong | 89 |
| 5 | Small-$K$ approx without 5% verification | May need quadratic | 94 |
| 6 | Molar mass in g/mol for $v_{\text{rms}} = \sqrt{3RT/M}$ | $v_{\text{rms}}$ wrong by ~×32 | 30 |
| 7 | $\Delta n$ in $K_p = K_c(RT)^{\Delta n}$ counts all species | $K_p$ wrong | 90 |
| 8 | Only $T$ changes $K$ — forgetting this in Le Chatelier | Misapplied shift direction | 99 |
| 9 | Henderson-Hasselbalch outside valid ratio (0.1–10) | pH unreliable | 112 |
| 10 | Equivalence point pH = 7 for weak acid/base | pH off by several units | 115, 116 |
| 11 | van't Hoff $i$ omitted for ionic solutes | Colligative properties too small | 144 |
| 12 | Oxidation number of O = −2 in peroxides / with F | Redox balancing wrong | 122 |
